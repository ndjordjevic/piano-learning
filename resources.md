## 1. Boogie-Woogie

### Todd Lowry — *Boogie-Woogie Piano* (Hal Leonard Keyboard Style Series, 2013)
`Todd Lowry - Boogie-Woogie Piano/`
- 📕 72 pp — `Todd Lowry - Boogie-Woogie Piano.pdf` (12 MB).
- 🎧 37 tracks (`Track01–37.mp3`). **LH on the left channel, RH on the right**, so you can practise hands separately by muting one side.
  - Tracks 1–28 at 100 bpm, 29–44 at 120 bpm, 45–55 at 140 bpm. Style File songs are each recorded twice: slow (100) and at full tempo (160–170).
- Contents: what boogie-woogie is → history → harmonic structure → **bass lines** (chordal, walking, single-note, double-note) → RH rhythmic chord patterns → RH melodic riffs → special techniques (grace notes, tremolos, glissandos, clusters) → intros, breaks and endings → playing popular melodies in boogie style → writing your own boogie → **Style File** (6 complete pieces in the styles of the great players).

### Colin Davey & Frank Poloney — *Learn Boogie Woogie Piano* (1999)
`Colin Davey, Frank Poloney - Learn Boogie Woogie Piano - 1999 (pdf + mp3)/`
- 📕 122 pp — `Colin Davey, Frank Poloney - Learn Boogie Woogie Piano - 1999.pdf` (9.9 MB, 600 dpi scan)
- 🎧 92 tracks. The audio is organised as a **drill matrix**: each RH technique is played over 4 LH bass lines — *Basic*, *Monday Struggle*, *Death Ray Boogie* and *Roll 'Em Pete*:
  - 01–04 bass lines alone
  - 05–24 Rhythmic-Chord Ex. 1–5
  - 25–44 Pickup-Putdown Ex. 1–5
  - 45–64 Two-Bar Figure Ex. 1–5
  - 65–76 Combination Ex. 1–9
  - 77–84 Turnaround Ex. 1–5
  - 85–91 Figures from chapters 9–16
  - 92 *The Basic Boogie* (complete piece)
- Focus: **hand independence**.

### Christian Fuchs — *The Joy of Easy Boogie Woogie* Vol. 1 & 2
`Christian Fuchs/The Joy of Easy Boogie Woogie Vol.1.pdf` (22 pp), `... Vol. 2.pdf` (21 pp)
- 📕 Easy multi-chorus boogie pieces in C. Each PDF lists **YouTube video links** for the parts (Vol. 1: 3 videos).

### David Bennett Cohen — *Learn to Play Blues Piano* DVD 3: *Boogie Woogie and Slow Blues* (2008)
→ see section 2.

## 2. Blues Piano

### David Bennett Cohen — *Teaches Blues Piano* Vol. 1 & 2 (Homespun, 1998)
`David Bennett Cohen - Teaches Blues Piano [1998]/Volume One|Volume Two/`
- 📕 Vol. 1 booklet, 25 pp · Vol. 2 booklet, 44 pp
- 🎧 139 audio lessons in total (spoken explanation plus examples)
- **Vol. 1**: 12-bar form, I-IV-V, LH rhythm, turnarounds, shuffle rhythm, back-beat accents, alternate and syncopated shuffle rhythms (quintuplet, triplet), modular 12-bar, Solos 1–3, diminished-chord rhythms, 9th-chord rhythm, C minor blues scale, improvisation ideas, **slow blues** (LH patterns, chord variations, melodic ideas, common riffs), endings.
- **Vol. 2**: straight-8th rhythm and 8 alternates, 9th-chord 8th rhythm, solos, **walking bass**, **Fats Domino bass line**, quick-IV, **boogie-woogie bass line** with soloing, straight-time boogie, **New Orleans rumba bass lines**, **13 turnarounds**, **7 endings**.

### David Bennett Cohen — *Learn to Play Blues Piano* DVDs (video)
- 🎬 `David Cohen - Learn To Play Blues Piano 01 - [2004 Version].avi` (684 MB)
- 🎬 `David Cohen - Learn To Play Blues Piano 02 - [2004 Version].avi` (639 MB)
- `David Bennett Cohen - Learn to Play Blues Piano 3-4 (2008)/`
  - 🎬 DVD 3 — **Boogie Woogie and Slow Blues** + 📕 12-page booklet
  - 🎬 DVD 4 — **New Orleans Style Made Easy** + 📕 16-page booklet
- DVDs 1–2 cover similar ground to the Homespun audio course above; 3–4 cover boogie/slow blues and New Orleans.

### Todd Lowry — *Best of Blues Piano* (Hal Leonard)
`Todd Lowry - Best of Blues Piano/`
- 📕 51 pp · 🎧 36 tracks in `CD/`, most with a **full-band** and a **slow-demo** version
- **Repertoire / transcriptions** in the style of classic recordings: *The Pearls*, *Cow Cow Blues*, *Cryin' In My Sleep*, *(The Original) Boogie Woogie*, *Roll 'Em Pete*, *Honky Tonk Train*, *Confessin' the Blues*, *Everyday I Have the Blues*, *Route 66*, *Diving Duck*, *Caldonia*, *Big Chief*, *Blueberry Hill*, *Tipitina*.

### Mark Harrison — *Blues Piano: The Complete Guide* (Hal Leonard, 2003)
`Mark Harrison - Blues Piano The Complete Guide [HL] [2003, PDF, MP3@128, ENG]/`
- 📕 `Mark Harrison - Blues Piano The Complete Guide.pdf` (82 pp incl. covers, 80 numbered)
- 🎧 94 tracks (`blues piano [audio 94 tracks] - MP3@128/`)
- Blues reference: scales, 12-bar forms, voicings, LH patterns, comping and soloing across blues styles.

### Christian Fuchs — *Blues Piano for Beginners* 1–3, *Easy Blues Piano Bundle*, *Left Hands for Beginners*
`Christian Fuchs/`
- 📕 *Blues Piano For Beginners* 1, 2, 3 (21, 21 and 23 pp)
- 📕 *Easy Blues Piano Bundle Series Vol 1+2+3* (96 pp). Contains YouTube links for Parts I–IV, e.g. Part I https://youtu.be/LSDXXjCpPSA
