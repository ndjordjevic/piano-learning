## 4. Left Hands for Beginners — Phase 2 (alongside the Easy Blues course)
`Left Hands For Beginners.pdf` · video with all left hands: https://youtu.be/WyXxUBSZXcs

Method (from the book):
1. Play the pattern on each chord (I, IV, V) separately.
2. Play it over the 12-bar blues form.
3. Practise the chord jumps separately until they're clean.
4. Add simple right-hand ideas on top.

Every pattern works swung and straight.
